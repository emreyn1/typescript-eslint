HA VEN HUB-ONLINESHOP
Founded in 2013, Genie Collection/Haven Hub started with a
simple vision: to bring quality fragrances to the community. From
its humble beginnings in street-to-street selling, the brand
quickly gained recognition in 2014 at the Al Quoz weekly
market. By 2015, Genie Collection entered Burjuman Mall,
achieving daily sales averaging AED 3,000. Building on this
success, the brand expanded across the UAE with outlets in
Burjuman Mall, Al Ghurair Centre, Reef Mall, Dubai Festival City
Mall, Abu Dhabi Mall, Al Ain, and Fujairah. The company has
since diversified into skincare and Himalayan wellness products,
offering a holistic range to customers. Alongside Genie
Collection, AA Collection was launched in 2015, further
strengthening the brand’s presence. After the pandemic, the
business adapted to digital transformation with the launch of
Haven Hub Onlineshop, making products accessible nationwide
through e-commerce. Today, Genie Collection stands as a
trusted lifestyle brand in the UAE, built on dedication,
innovation, and customer trust.
